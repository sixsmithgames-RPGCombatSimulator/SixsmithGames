## Campaign idea

### The problem

Your campaign notes know why the villain matters. Your VTT knows where the
villain is standing. In the middle of a session, the GM is the person forced to
hold those two halves together.

### The useful promise

GameMaster Studio gives each half of the job a clear home: campaign memory in
GameMasterCraft and encounter control in Virtual Combat Simulator.

### What we can prove

- GameMasterCraft organizes NPCs, factions, locations, lore, timelines, session
  notes, and campaign continuity.
- VCS puts maps, tokens, initiative, hit points, conditions, and combat controls
  in the encounter workspace.
- GameMaster Studio is $14.99 per month.
- GameMasterCraft AI and VCS GM remain separate $9.99 monthly subscriptions.
- The server-side handoff foundation exists, but the customer-facing handoff is
  not yet finished.
- Today, the GM chooses what encounter results become campaign history.

### What must not appear

- automatic GMC-to-VCS sync;
- automatic campaign updates after combat;
- a claim that both products already behave as one finished customer interface;
- invented customer quotes, user counts, time-saving numbers, or reviews;
- any public mention of the hidden owner-only product.

## 90-second OBS recording script

### Recording setup

- Record at 1920 × 1080 and 30 FPS.
- Use a clean browser profile with demo campaign data.
- Record the microphone on its own track.
- Capture in MKV, then remux the chosen take to MP4.
- Keep the mouse still unless it is pointing to the feature being discussed.
- Do not show email addresses, payment data, browser bookmarks, or private keys.
